FAQ
Mobile mechanic questions

        

          
What does mobile mechanic service mean?
Christian's Auto Repair brings a service truck to the vehicle and performs suitable diagnostics, maintenance, and repairs on-site instead of requiring a traditional shop visit.

          
Where can service be performed?
At your home, driveway, workplace, or another agreed location where the vehicle can be accessed safely and legally. Final location suitability is confirmed before service.

          
Can every repair be done mobile?
Many repairs can be handled on-site, but not every job is appropriate for field service. Send the vehicle details and symptoms so Christian's Auto Repair can review the job.

          
Do I need to know what's wrong before I request service?
No. Describe what the vehicle is doing, including warning lights, noises, leaks, and whether it starts or drives. Diagnosis can begin from the symptoms.

          
Do I need to provide tools or equipment?
Do not assume you need to provide anything beyond access to the vehicle unless Christian's Auto Repair asks for something specific when confirming the appointment.

          
Can I request same-day help?
You can ask about urgent or same-day availability. Availability must be confirmed directly and is not guaranteed by the website.

          
Where is my service-request draft stored?
Until direct form delivery is connected, the request draft is stored only in your current browser so you can review or copy it without retyping. You can clear the saved draft at any time.
