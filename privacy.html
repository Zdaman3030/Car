PRIVACY & LOCAL STORAGE

What this website stores and shares

This site is intentionally built without a customer database or paid backend. Most information stays in your browser unless you choose to share it.

Saved on your device

Service-request drafts, saved vehicles, and contact details you explicitly choose to remember are stored in your browser's local storage on that device. They are not automatically uploaded to Christian's Auto Repair.

VIN decoding

When you use VIN decoding, the VIN is sent to the public NHTSA vPIC service so available vehicle details can be returned. Manual vehicle entry remains available if you prefer not to use VIN decoding.

Photos

Selected photos remain on your device unless you choose a Share action that includes them. The website does not automatically upload photos to a server.

Service assistant

The current service assistant runs in your browser using local logic. Your chat messages are not sent to a paid generative-AI API by this website.

Submitting a request

The website currently prepares a service-request summary for copy, share, text, email, print, or download. A request is not automatically submitted or confirmed until you send it using one of those methods after Christian's contact information is configured.

Clearing saved information

You can clear the saved service draft and saved contact details from the Request Service section. Saved vehicles can also be removed individually. Clearing this site's browser storage will remove all locally saved website data.

No tracking claim is being made beyond the site's current code. This page describes how the current website is implemented. It does not invent legal, retention, warranty, or business policies that Christian has not provided.
